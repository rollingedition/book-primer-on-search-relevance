# WANDS sample

The data every example in this book reads: a sample of WANDS, the Wayfair ANnotation DataSet, a public
product-search dataset with human relevance judgments. Generated by `prep/wands_sample.py`; never edit
these files by hand.

**Source:** https://github.com/wayfair/WANDS, commit `3b74dcf4ba29ab8ff3e6a50b5b09fc627cb882b5`.
**Licence:** MIT, Copyright (c) 2021 ecir2022. See [`LICENSE`](LICENSE), copied from the source repository.
**Paper:** Chen, Y., Liu, S., Liu, Z., Sun, W., Baltrunas, L. and Schroeder, B. (2022). WANDS: Dataset for
Product Search Relevance Assessment. ECIR 2022.

| File | Rows | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `products.csv` | 12,000 | `product_id`, `name`, `product_class`, `category` (a path such as "Furniture / Bedroom Furniture / Beds & Headboards / Beds"), `description` (cut to about 400 characters), `attributes` (see below), `rating_count`, `average_rating`, `review_count` |
| `queries.csv` | 80 | `query_id`, `query`, `query_class`, `split`: 50 `train` queries for tuning, 30 `test` queries for reporting |
| `labels.csv` | 13,077 | `query_id`, `product_id`, `label` (`exact`, `partial` or `irrelevant`) and `grade` (2, 1 or 0): one row per pair |
| `label_conflicts.csv` | 7 | Pairs WANDS judged twice with different labels: `query_id`, `product_id`, `labels` (both, joined by `|`). `labels.csv` keeps the lower grade |

Labels: 3,252 exact, 7,972 partial, 1,853 irrelevant. Judged products per query: 61 to 299, median 158.

**Attributes:** WANDS's `product_features` holds thousands of keys, mostly warranties and compliance. `attributes`
keeps a few shopping attributes, as "name: value" pairs separated by "; ": color, material, style, finish, shape, size, width, height, depth. Values are as WANDS gives them;
it doesn't state units for the dimensions. 11,851 products have at least one attribute.

**How it was drawn** (seed 2026): queries with 60 to 300 judged products, at least 3 exact and
20 partial judgments, at most one per query class; 80 of them, split 50 train and
30 test. 4 queries that name the store, its own brands or a person were swapped
for the next in the same order. Products: every product judged for a chosen query, plus randomly chosen unjudged
products to make 12,000. A product that wasn't judged for a query is unjudged for it, not irrelevant.
